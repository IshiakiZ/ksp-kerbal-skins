# Kerbal Skins

The colour of the kerbals' skin and (since 0.2.0) their hair and eyes: one colour for everyone, a different one for each
kerbal, or your own choice for any one of them. Mouths and suits stay as they are.

![Five kerbals in the game's own green, then each in a colour of their own as the mod is switched on](src/KerbalSkins/img/kerbal-skins.gif)

For Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x. **Needs [Keystone](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-keystone)**, a separate download
([on SpaceDock](https://spacedock.info/mod/4665/Keystone)): this mod does not load without it.

## Install

Copy the `KerbalSkins` folder from the download into your KSP's `GameData`, beside `Keystone`.

## Use

Open the Keystone window (the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Alt-K; Option-K on a Mac), turn to
**Kerbal Skins** and switch on **Change skin colours**. Set one colour for everyone, let each kerbal have their own,
or click a kerbal's colour box to choose theirs. A kerbal's own colour is remembered by name, in every save.

## Good to know

* It works wherever kerbals are drawn (0.2.0): in flight, on the opening screen, among the assembly building's ground
  crew and in the astronaut complex. Heads from other mods (0.2.0: TextureReplacer's head packs and the like) are coloured
  too, by their own skin's hues; their hair is left as drawn. Those with no crew record (the opening screen's, the ground
  crew) take a colour of their own when each has their own.
* With **Each their own** on, a kerbal newly hired is given one of the colours at random, kept by name from then on.
  `people's` (0.2.0) gives people's own skin: the ten tones of the Monk Skin Tone Scale (Ellis Monk and Google, CC BY 4.0),
  each kerbal a little lighter, darker, warmer or cooler than the tone (with Keystone 0.11.0, how much so is a finer detail in
  its Advanced view).
* **Their own hair too** and **their own eye colour too** (0.2.0): with each their own, every kerbal's hair and eyes are
  their own colours as well, kept by name, in the shares people's come in. Any kerbal's can be chosen on the page.

## Works on

Mac: tried. Windows 11: 0.1.3 tried; what 0.2.0 adds, not yet. Linux: never run (it is plain C#, with nothing of one
system's in it).
