# Choosing colours

Open the Keystone window (the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or the modifier key and K:
Option-K on a Mac, Alt-K elsewhere) and turn to **Kerbal Skins**.

![The mod's page, with one kerbal's colour being chosen](../src/KerbalSkins/img/skins-picker.jpg)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Change skin colours | off: every kerbal is the game's own green again |
| Everyone | the colour of every kerbal who has not been given one of their own. Click the colour box (or `change`) to choose it |
| Each their own | `no`, or give every kerbal without a colour of their own a different one, always the same for the same name: `greens` (eight shades of green) or `any colour` (sixteen, from greens through blue and pink to tan and dark brown) |
| The kerbals here | those aboard the ship being flown and those outside it. Click a kerbal's colour box (or `change`) to choose a colour for that kerbal; `as the rest` takes a kerbal's own colour away again; `done` closes the chooser |

## The chooser

* **The row of ready colours**: the sixteen that `any colour` deals out. Click one.
* **The square**: every shade of one colour, paler to the left, darker downwards. Click or drag in it; the
  kerbal changes as you go.
* **The strip under it**: which colour the square is shades of. Click or drag along it.
* **The box with six letters and digits**: the colour as it is written for a web page (`C29174`, say). Type
  one and press Enter. While you are typing there, the game's own keys are held off, so an M does not open
  the map.

(Until version 0.1.1 the chooser was three sliders: hue, strength and light.)

A kerbal's own colour is remembered by name, in every save.
