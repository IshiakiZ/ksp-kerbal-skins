# Limits

* Flight only. The kerbals on the game's opening screen are not changed (the astronaut complex and the
  crew lists show no faces).
* Tried on 2026-10-07 with the stock heads: a man and a woman in their seats, the portraits, and a man
  outside the ship. Not tried with mods that give kerbals other heads (TextureReplacer and its head
  packs): a head whose picture has another name is left alone.
* The skin is found by its colour. A head pack whose skin is not the stock yellow-green would not be
  recoloured.
* **Windows and Linux:** it is plain C# and asks only the game itself for things (no shader, no library
  of its own), so it should work there as on a Mac; it has been run on a Mac only.
